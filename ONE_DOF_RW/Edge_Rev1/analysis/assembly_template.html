    Parts

      Total about 133 g, which includes 3 g for the cable's drag. Untick a part to look at what is behind it.

      The geometry is the exported CAD; the kit's board, connectors and standoffs are envelopes. Their sizes come from the listing's drawings, except the standoff lengths (7 mm), which are scaled off its photos and not measured: the unit may be up to 2.9 mm longer than drawn before anything leaves the 44 mm depth. The alignment pin is not drawn. Nothing is printed yet, and the glued frame joint is untested.
